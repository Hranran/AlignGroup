# Third-party code

AlignGroup is built on [OvSGTR](https://github.com/gpt4vision/OvSGTR) and
[Grounding DINO](https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO).
The repository also contains upstream notices for DETR, Conditional DETR,
DINO, and their dependencies. Existing copyright notices and license files
are retained. See the root `LICENSE` and `GroundingDINO/LICENSE`.

CMRA and SRG are the AlignGroup components. Grounding DINO, deformable
attention, multi-head attention, matching, and the inherited distillation
pipeline are credited to their respective prior work.

Dataset images, benchmark annotations, pretrained detector/language-model
weights, and experiment outputs are not included. Obtain these from their
providers under the applicable terms.
